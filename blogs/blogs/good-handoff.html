The gentle art of a good handoff

Small rituals that help a growing team keep its work human.

11.01.263 min read

A handoff is more than a file moving from one person to another. It is context, care, and a little trust packaged in a form someone else can use.

We have started treating the handoff as part of the work itself: explain the why, mark the open questions, and leave enough room for the next person to make it their own.

Leave the next person a clear path, not a puzzle.
