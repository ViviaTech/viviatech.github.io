What building in public taught us

A few honest lessons from sharing the work before it was finished.

18.01.264 min read

Sharing early work is uncomfortable in a useful way. It makes assumptions visible, and it gives good feedback enough time to change the shape of an idea.

We have learned that building in public is less about performance and more about keeping the door open. Show the draft. Ask the real question. Let the next version be better because someone joined the process.

Progress likes company

The work is still ours to make, but it becomes more thoughtful when it is made in conversation with the people it hopes to serve.
