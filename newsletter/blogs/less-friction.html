Less friction, more possibility

Small improvements can open surprisingly large doors.

22.01.263 min read

We tend to notice friction only after it has accumulated: the extra click, the unclear label, the task that takes three tries. One moment is small. A hundred moments shape a relationship.

Our craft is often the work of looking for those moments and making them lighter. Not through decoration, but through attention: to sequence, language, contrast, and the quiet logic of a person trying to get something done.

The smallest useful change is still a change in someone’s day.
