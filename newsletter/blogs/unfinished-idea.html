Make room for the unfinished idea

Why the most useful thinking often starts out a little unclear.

05.01.264 min read

Some ideas arrive polished. The important ones usually arrive with edges missing. They need a little space before they can tell us what they are trying to become.

We try to protect that early stage at ViVia. Not every thought needs a meeting, a roadmap, or a verdict. Sometimes the best next move is to let it breathe long enough for a better question to appear.

Curiosity before certainty

Making room for the unfinished is not a refusal to decide. It is a way of making the eventual decision with more of the picture in view.
