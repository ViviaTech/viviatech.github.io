Inside our first open studio

An afternoon of sketches, questions, and the ideas that only appear together.

28.01.264 min read

There is a special energy in putting unfinished work on a table. It makes room for questions before answers have settled, and lets a good idea borrow momentum from the people around it.

At our first open studio, designers, builders, friends, and neighbours moved through the room with that generous kind of curiosity. We left with new notes in the margins and a much clearer sense of what deserves our attention next.

Keep the door open

The studio is at its best when it is porous. We plan to keep making space for conversations that sharpen the work and broaden who it is for.
