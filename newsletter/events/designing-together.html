Designing for everyone, together

What happened when we invited our neighbours into the product conversation.

21.01.263 min read

We brought a few early ideas to the community and asked a simple question: where does this feel useful, and where does it get in the way?

The answers were practical, surprising, and exactly what a product needs before it becomes too certain of itself. People pointed to the moments that felt smooth, the moments that felt cold, and the opportunities we had not noticed yet.

Good design gets stronger when more people can leave a mark on it.
