Three days at Future Forum

The moments and questions we are still carrying home from this year’s gathering.

12.01.265 min read

Future Forum was full of big ideas, but the most useful conversations were often the smallest ones: a late coffee, a note shared between sessions, a question that stayed unresolved.

We came home thinking less about predictions and more about practice. What can we make clearer today? What would make the next person’s work a little easier? Those are the questions worth keeping.
