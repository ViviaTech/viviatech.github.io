Tools change quickly. Principles should be a little more durable. As we plan the next wave of ViVia products, we keep returning to three ideas: make it clear, make it flexible, and make it matter.

That means thoughtful defaults, room to adapt, and a stubborn respect for the time people spend with our work. The details will evolve. The north star stays put.

> Build for the moment someone needs it, not the moment someone presents it.
